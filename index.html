About

A single, dependable face for brands across the Gulf — elegant on camera, credible in the room.

Pardis works fluidly between editorial and commercial worlds: the close-up precision of a luxury watch and hand campaign, the movement of modest couture and abaya, the polish of a beauty shoot, and the poise of representing a house on set and at events. Bilingual and GCC-based, she brings one consistent, professional presence to every collaboration.
